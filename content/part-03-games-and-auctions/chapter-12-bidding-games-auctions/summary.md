Section 12 analyzes sequential bidding games (Richman, whole-chip parity, raise ladders) and sealed-bid mechanism design.

Key ideas:
- Richman costs satisfy $R(v) = \frac{1}{2}(\max R(w) + \min R(w))$ and equal random-turn losing probabilities.
- With whole chips and a tie-break token, $u = 2a + t$ makes the token worth exactly half a chip and turns $2N + 1$ odd so final rounds never tie.
- Raise ladders up to $m$ toward $T$ obey modulo $m + 1$ subtraction-game residues.
- Second-price, first-price, and all-pay auctions with $n$ i.i.d. $\operatorname{Uniform}[0,1]$ private values all share expected revenue $\frac{n-1}{n+1}$ by the Envelope theorem.
- In common-value settings, conditioning on winning or seller acceptance exposes the winner's curse.
