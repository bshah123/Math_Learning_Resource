Section 15 solves five classic three-point geometric problems using determinants, linear systems, $60^\circ$ rotations, circular arcs, and disjoint semicircle events.

Key ideas:
- Borehole elevations fix a plane $z = px + qy + r$ with dip $\tan\delta = \sqrt{p^2 + q^2}$ and perpendicular strike.
- Rotating $\triangle APB$ by $60^\circ$ proves the Fermat point condition ($120^\circ$ angles when all triangle angles $< 120^\circ$, else the obtuse vertex).
- Snellius–Pothenot resection loses uniqueness whenever the observer sits on the circumcircle of the three landmarks (danger circle).
- $n$ uniform random points on a circle all lie in some semicircle with probability $n/2^{n-1}$, making a random inscribed triangle acute with probability $1/4$.

Before moving on, make sure you can:
- Derive the Fermat point $60^\circ$ rotation proof and recall the obtuse-angle exception.
- Prove the $1 - n/2^{n-1}$ probability for $n$ circle points containing the center.
- Identify the danger circle in resection and the collinearity determinant test.
