Section 14 turns 2D/3D geometry into vector algebra, lattice counting, and polyhedral combinatorics.

Key ideas:
- Plane normals $n = (B-A)\times(C-A)$, point-plane distance, and De Gua's 3D Pythagorean theorem govern 3D triangles and tetrahedra.
- Pick's theorem $\text{Area} = I + B/2 - 1$ rules out equilateral lattice triangles in $\mathbb{R}^2$.
- $(p-2)(q-2) < 4$ restricts Platonic solids to five; equal-edge octahedra have $4\times$ tetrahedron volume and complementary dihedral angles ($70.53^\circ + 109.47^\circ = 180^\circ$).
