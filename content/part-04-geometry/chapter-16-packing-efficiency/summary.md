Section 16 derives planar, cubic, interstitial, and polyhedral packing efficiencies from unit-cell geometry.

Key ideas:
- Fractional shared-atom counting ($1/8$ corner, $1/4$ edge, $1/2$ face) gives SC ($1$), BCC ($2$), and FCC ($4$) spheres per cell, yielding densities $\pi/6$, $\pi\sqrt{3}/8$, and $\pi/(3\sqrt{2})$.
- FCC and HCP both contain $N$ octahedral voids ($r/R = \sqrt{2}-1$) and $2N$ tetrahedral voids ($r/R = \sqrt{3/2}-1$).
- Regular octahedra ($109.47^\circ$) and tetrahedra ($70.53^\circ$) fill space together in $2/3$ and $1/3$ volume shares; Minkowski's best single-shape octahedron lattice reaches $18/19$.
