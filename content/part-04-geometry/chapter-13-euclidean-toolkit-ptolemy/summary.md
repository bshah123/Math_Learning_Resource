Section 13 gathers the core Euclidean triangle/circle theorems together with Ptolemy's theorem and inequality.

Key ideas:
- $K = rs = abc/(4R)$, $OH = 3\cdot OG$, $OI^2 = R(R - 2r)$, and Stewart/Ceva/Menelaus handle triangle metrical geometry.
- Circle angle chasing and Power of a Point $PX\cdot PY = |OP^2 - \rho^2|$ convert chords and tangents into algebra.
- Ptolemy's identity $(a-c)(b-d) = (a-b)(c-d) + (a-d)(b-c)$ gives $AC \cdot BD \le AB \cdot CD + AD \cdot BC$ with equality iff concyclic/collinear in order.

Before moving on, make sure you can:
- Derive the golden ratio in a pentagon and $1/a = 1/b + 1/c$ in a heptagon via Ptolemy.
- Prove both directions of Pompeiu's theorem for an equilateral triangle.
- Compute $r, R, OI^2$ for any Heronian triangle.
