Section 2 equips you to prove or disprove Hamiltonian cycles when no simple degree formula characterizes them.

Key ideas:
- Prove non-existence via the components test ($c(G - S) \le |S|$), bipartite color balance, or forced degree-2 edges.
- Prove existence via Ore's condition ($\deg u + \deg v \ge n$ for non-adjacent pairs), Dirac's condition ($\delta \ge n/2$), or explicit Gray-code constructions on $Q_n$.
- Every tournament contains a directed Hamiltonian path (Rédei's theorem).
- Pósa's inner/outer argument combines knight chessboard alternation with row alternation to rule out closed $4 \times n$ tours.

Before moving on, make sure you can:
- Reproduce the edge-maximal path-reversal proof of Ore's theorem.
- Compute the $k$-th Gray codeword using $k \oplus \lfloor k/2 \rfloor$.
- Explain why $\binom{n-1}{2} + 2$ edges force Hamiltonicity while $\binom{n-1}{2} + 1$ does not.
