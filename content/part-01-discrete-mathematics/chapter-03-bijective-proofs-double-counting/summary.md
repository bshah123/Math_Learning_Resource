Section 3 turns enumeration problems into invertible bijections and two-way incidence counts.

Key ideas:
- Reflection across $y = x + 1$ proves the Catalan number $C_n = \frac{1}{n+1}\binom{2n}{n}$ and the ballot theorem $(a-b)/(a+b)$.
- Prüfer codes biject labeled trees on $[n]$ with $[n]^{n-2}$ where vertex $v$ appears $\deg(v)-1$ times, proving Cayley's $n^{n-2}$.
- Ferrers conjugation swaps part count with maximum part; Glaisher's binary factorization bijects odd partitions with distinct partitions.

- Explain why dividing by symmetry fails when orbits have unequal sizes.
