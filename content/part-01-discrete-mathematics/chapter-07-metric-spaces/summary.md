Section 7 builds metric intuition across $\ell_p$ spaces, graphs, $p$-adics, trees, and coding bounds.

Key ideas:
- On $\mathbb{R}^n$, $\|x\|_\infty \le \|x\|_2 \le \|x\|_1 \le \sqrt{n}\|x\|_2 \le n\|x\|_\infty$.
- Ultrametrics obey $d(x,z) \le \max(d(x,y), d(y,z))$, forcing every triangle to be isosceles with its two longest sides equal.
- Every maximal $\varepsilon$-separated set is an $\varepsilon$-net; volume comparison bounds codewords by $(1 + 2/\varepsilon)^n$ in $\mathbb{R}^n$ and $2^n / V(n, t)$ in Hamming space.
- Hamming $(n=7, t=1)$ and binary Golay $(n=23, t=3)$ are perfect codes hitting the sphere-packing bound with equality.
