Section 5 brings together hypergraphs, structural automorphisms via Orbit-Stabilizer, and exact symmetry counting via Burnside and Pólya.

Key ideas:
- Hypergraph automorphisms preserve degrees and incidences, though combinatorial symmetries can strictly exceed geometric symmetries ($32$ vs $8$ on the $4\times 4$ tic-tac-toe board).
- The Fano plane has $|\operatorname{Aut}| = 7 \cdot 6 \cdot 4 = 168$.
- Burnside's lemma averages $|\operatorname{Fix}(g)|$ over $g \in G$; Pólya sets $|\operatorname{Fix}(g)| = k^{c(g)}$ or substitutes $x_i = r^i + b^i$ into the cycle index.

Before moving on, make sure you can:
- Prove Burnside's lemma by double-counting $(g, x)$ with $gx = x$.
- Compute necklace, bracelet, and cube face coloring counts from cycle structures.
- Distinguish when geometric symmetries are only a proper subgroup of $\operatorname{Aut}(H)$.
