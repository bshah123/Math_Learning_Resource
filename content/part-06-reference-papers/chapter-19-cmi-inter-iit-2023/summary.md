Chapter 19 covers all 5 Olympiad problems from the CMI Inter-IIT Tech Fest Mathematical Competition 2023.

Key ideas:
- Testing the unit all-ones vector $\mathbf{1}/\sqrt{n}$ in the Rayleigh quotient bounds $\lambda_{\max}$ for near-constant symmetric matrices (CMI P1).
- Pairing $x_i = \sqrt{a}$ with $y_i = \sqrt{a(2b+1)}$ collapses cyclic root sums via Cauchy–Schwarz (CMI P2).
- Countability lets you dominate $f: \mathbb{Q}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ recursively, while $\mathbb{R} \cong \mathbb{N}^\mathbb{N}$ breaks any $g(x)+g(y)$ upper bound by diagonalization (CMI P3).
- Hall's Marriage Theorem extracts independent sets across red/blue connected components (CMI P4), and primorials $+1$ with $p_N / N \to \infty$ build infinite deficient sumsets (CMI P5).

Before moving on, make sure you can:
- Apply Hall's Marriage Theorem to two partitions of $[m\ell]$ into size-$\ell$ blocks.
- Explain why $(1 + 1/p_N)^N \to 1$ as $N \to \infty$.
