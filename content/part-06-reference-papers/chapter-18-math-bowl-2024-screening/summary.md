Chapter 18 provides full competition solutions to all 18 problems of the Math Bowl 2024 Screening Test.

Key ideas:
- Symmetry substitutions ($x \mapsto 1-x$) plus AM-GM/Cauchy-Schwarz crack integral and cyclic rational inequalities (P1, P4).
- Real Schur triangularization and rank-1 trace shifts $\operatorname{rk}(C+I)=1 \implies v^{\mathsf{T}}u = n$ solve the matrix problems (P5–P7).
- Swapping walkers give increasing trails of length $\ge n-1$ in $K_n$ (P3); indicator covariance gives $\operatorname{Var}(S) = 1$ (P17); degree-weighted vertex sums form a martingale for simultaneous graph rumor spread (P18).
- Highest-$2^r$ valuation blocks (P13), Bertrand's postulate squeezing (P14), and subgroup factorizations $T^{504}-1$ with Quadratic Reciprocity (P16) close the number theory problems.

- Derive $\operatorname{tr}(ABAB) - \operatorname{tr}(A^2B^2) = \frac{1}{2}n(n-1)$ from $C = uv^{\mathsf{T}} - I$.

- Solve simultaneous voter-model absorption via the degree-weighted martingale.
