# Math Learning Resource — Integral Cup 2026 & Inter-IIT Math Bowl

Standalone interactive pure-mathematics study guide, Olympiad proof archive, and interactive lab built for **Integral Cup 2026** and **Inter-IIT Math Bowl** preparation.

- **Live Site (GitHub Pages)**: https://bshah123.github.io/Math_Learning_Resource/
- **Contents**:
  - **Part 0 — Cross-Cutting Toolkit & Exam Blueprint** (`§0.1`–`§0.3`)
  - **Part I — Discrete Mathematics** (`§1`–`§8`: Graphs, Hamiltonicity, Bijections, Prüfer Codes, Homomorphisms, Burnside–Pólya, Coloring, Ramsey Theory, Metric Spaces, Optimization)
  - **Part II — Number Theory** (`§9`: Legendre, Kummer, Bertrand's Postulate, Prime Bounds)
  - **Part III — Games & Auctions** (`§10`–`§12`: Nash, Minimax, Poker Bluffing, Loopy Retrograde Analysis, Richman Bidding, Revenue Equivalence)
  - **Part IV — Geometry & Packing** (`§13`–`§16`: Ptolemy, Vectors, De Gua, Pick's Theorem, Platonic Solids, Fermat Point, Sphere Packings)
  - **Part V — Cross-Topic Bridges** (`§17`)
  - **Part VI — Past Reference Papers Solved** (`§18` Math Bowl 2024 Screening Test + `§19` CMI Inter-IIT 2023)
